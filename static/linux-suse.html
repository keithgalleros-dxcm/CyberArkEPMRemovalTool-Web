Removal readiness

  1. Identify the CyberArk product name, package name and installed version from your software inventory or administrator.
  2. Record the Linux distribution and version shown above. Verify that version against the product's support matrix.
  3. Get the documented uninstall procedure and any required approval or secure token from your CyberArk administrator.
  4. Plan a maintenance window and test the procedure on a disposable endpoint before production use.

No package removal commands are generated. EPM, Identity and Privilege products have different removal procedures.
